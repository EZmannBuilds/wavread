## Loudness

Integrated loudness in LUFS, following ITU-R BS.1770. Loudness range in LU, following EBU Tech 3342. True peak in dBTP, estimated with 4× oversampling as BS.1770 describes. Loudness is also measured across the track, so a quiet second chorus shows up as a quiet second chorus rather than a slightly lower average.

## Frequency balance

A 1/3-octave spectrum, grouped into named regions, each reported as its share of the measured energy. For example:

> 34.9% of measured energy sits in the low mids, the largest share of any band.

## Stereo

Stereo width and mono compatibility, measured over time. The report can show that the intro collapses in mono while the rest of the track holds up.

## What the report does not do

It does not grade your mix, and it does not decide what is wrong with it. There is no correct loudness, no correct balance and no correct width to measure against. A reference track is something to compare with, not a target.

Each finding gives the measurement, what it may mean, and what to listen for. You can keep the number and disagree with the reading.
