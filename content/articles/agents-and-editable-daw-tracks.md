## Not music from scratch

WavRead does not generate songs, stems or audio of any kind. It measures audio you already made. Everything here starts from a project open in your DAW, with tracks you can still change.

## The loop

1. Bounce the mix, or export aligned stems, from Ableton Live or FL Studio.
2. Analyze them in WavRead, on your own computer.
3. Give the agent the Track Document, or let it read the analysis through the WavRead MCP helper.
4. Ask a specific question about a specific part.
5. Make the change in your session, bounce again, and compare the two readings.

## What the agent can point at

With stems, WavRead measures every part on its own: its level, its peaks, where its energy sits and when it enters. So the agent can name one part in one section, with a timestamp, instead of talking about "the low end" in general.

The [WavRead skill](/agent.html) tells the agent which numbers were measured and which were estimated, and has it ask which DAW and setup you use before it suggests a step. What comes back is a starting point for your own tracks, confirmed by listening.

## What stays with you

WavRead never writes to your audio and does not control your DAW. The MCP helper talks only to WavRead. Every edit to the session is one you make, or one you hand to a separate tool you chose. The project stays a set of editable tracks the whole time.
