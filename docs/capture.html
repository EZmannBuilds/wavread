WavRead Bridge

Capture from your DAW.
Coming soon.

Direct DAW capture is coming soon and disabled in public builds. You can analyze your music today using the files you already export.

Bring the mix. Bring the parts.

  1. Export your finished mix from your DAW as WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3, M4A, or OGG.
  2. For a closer look, export aligned stems with the same start point.
  3. Open the mix or stem folder in WavRead to inspect levels, frequency balance, stereo, and the shape of the song.

WavRead analyzes the files you supply. It does not separate a finished mix into stems or change your audio.
