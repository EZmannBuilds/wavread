Owner controls

Licence controls

Grant a complimentary licence by email. They can sign in with that address and download WavRead without paying. This does not give them permission to grant licences.

Revoke and restore affect owner-issued free grants only. Paid purchases and contributor access are shown separately. Revocation stops future authorized downloads; it does not remotely disable an installed app.

No email is sent and no marketing signup is added. After granting, share wavread.com/signin with the creator. Granting the same address again will not duplicate their licence.

Problem reports

Linked devices

Linking connects a WavRead install to this account so its problem reports appear below. The app holds only a revocable report token — never your email or a session. Generate a code here, then enter it in WavRead under Settings → Problem reports.

Valid for 15 minutes, works once. Type it into the app; nothing else is needed.

Release notes

What changed in 1.5.4

  • A new listening workspace across the app, with a shared player and clearer navigation between your tracks, versions, references and stems.
  • Read the evidence, inspect the charts and listen to the relevant moment in one place. File and supplied-stem analysis remain available.
