Release notes

What changed in 1.4.46

  • Stems come from you now: WavRead no longer ships or fetches a separation model. Drop your DAW's exports in, or point WavRead at a tool you already have in Settings. The download is 70 MB smaller for it.
  • Analyze has two sides — File and Bridge — and an arming you set stays yours until you release it. Short captures are padded to the take instead of cut short.
  • Three questions at the welcome shape the readings: what you usually make, the closest genre, and your mixing experience. Any track can override the first two, and Download everything always contains every measurement regardless.
  • Capture the master beside your parts and the difference the bus makes is now audio you can play from the Mix tab, with its gain drawn over time on the Timeline.
  • Held vocal notes carry their tuning in cents, each part's level and timing are read across the whole take, and the spectral balance is drawn over time — including on analyses you already have.
  • The Export panel asks what you want, estimates the size, and has an Everything tick.
