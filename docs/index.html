How this comparison was made

“Unmeasured” is a constructed demonstration made from the existing v03 master and aligned stems. We lowered Memory Piano by 7 dB and Thread Cello by 3 dB, and raised Low Horizon by 5 dB, Gravity Sub by 10 dB, Bronze Footsteps by 5 dB, and Dust Pulse by 7 dB. The arrangement is the same. The adjusted reference mix already existed; this is not a historical before-and-after or a customer result.

Both full WAVs were measured with the same analysis settings in WavRead 1.5.4. The listening excerpts use constant gain to match integrated loudness, with no extra EQ or compression. WavRead analyzes the audio; it did not make these mix changes. View the measurement and playback details.

What can I analyze?

Open WAV, MP3, AIFF, FLAC, M4A, or OGG files. Analyze a finished mix, a batch of tracks, or aligned stems exported from your DAW.

Does WavRead change my sound?

WavRead analyzes and explains. It does not apply EQ, compression, mastering, or edits. Make your changes in your DAW, then bring back a new bounce.

Does my music leave my computer?

Audio analysis runs locally. Website purchases and downloads use an owner account. The app can check for updates, and optional problem reports require your consent. Read the privacy details.

Is this a subscription? Is there a trial?

It’s $50 once, with every 1.x update included. There is no trial of the current build. The purchase terms offer refunds requested within 30 days. A future major version may be a separate purchase.

Will it run on my computer?
