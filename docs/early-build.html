Five dollars, once

WavRead is a paid beta. Five dollars buys the software, every build while the beta runs, and a place where your bug reports actually land. One payment — no subscription, and it never renews.

Every beta build

New WavRead builds appear on your dashboard as they ship, each with its SHA-256 to verify against. Buying once covers the whole beta, not one version.

Your reports, tracked

Feedback and problem reports you send — from the website or from inside the app — are recorded against your account, with their status visible on your dashboard.

An account that lasts

Ownership is recorded against a durable WavRead account keyed to your email, not to a browser session. Sign-in is a one-time email link; there is no password.

On the record as early

Your purchase is dated and kept. When commercial 1.0 pricing is decided, early supporters are the first question, not an afterthought.

What five dollars is not

It is not a licence for the eventual 1.0, which has no promised date and no final price — though buying now is exactly the record that makes you an early supporter when that price is decided. It is not a subscription and never renews. And it does not buy a signed installer yet: beta builds are unsigned until WavRead has an Apple Developer account, so first launch needs one approval in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

WavRead is beta software sold as beta software: it measures carefully and says when it is estimating, but it is unfinished, and the honest reason the price is five dollars rather than fifty is that you are buying it early.
