What five dollars is not

It is not a subscription: nothing renews, and nothing charges you again on its own. It is also not a licence for the stable commercial build, which is a separate $49 purchase when it arrives — unless your reports earned it. What five dollars covers is the Early Launch: this build and every build after it while the Early Launch runs.

What it does buy is a build macOS trusts: WavRead is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarised by Apple. The first time you open it you get the ordinary "downloaded from the Internet — are you sure?" confirmation, which says in as many words that Apple checked it for malicious software and none was detected. Click Open and it never asks again. What you will not see is the refusal unsigned apps get, where there is no Open button at all.

WavRead is Early Launch software sold as exactly that: it measures carefully and says when it is estimating, it is still moving fast, and the honest reason the price is five dollars rather than fifty is that you are buying it early, one build at a time.
