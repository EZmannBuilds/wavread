Five dollars, early

The public beta stays free. The Early Build is for people who want the next build before it becomes the public one — and want their bug reports to land somewhere they can see. One payment of $5, no subscription.

Early-channel builds

New WavRead builds are published to the early channel first. Owners download them from their dashboard, verified by SHA-256, before the public release moves.

Your reports, tracked

Feedback and problem reports you send — from the website or from inside the app — are recorded against your account, with their status visible on your dashboard.

An account that lasts

Ownership is recorded against a durable WavRead account keyed to your email, not to a browser session. Sign-in is a one-time email link; there is no password.

On the record as early

Your purchase is dated and kept. When commercial 1.0 pricing is decided, early supporters are the first question, not an afterthought.

What five dollars is not

It is not a licence for the eventual 1.0, which has no promised date and no final price. It is not a subscription and never renews. It does not change the free public beta, which stays downloadable by everyone without an account. And it does not buy a signed installer yet: early builds, like the public beta, are unsigned until WavRead has an Apple Developer account, so first launch still needs one approval in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
