What fifty dollars is not

It is not a subscription: nothing renews, and nothing charges you again on its own. It is not a rental, and it is not a licence that expires — the build you download stays yours and keeps working whether or not you ever come back. There is no free version and no trial of the current build.

What it does buy is a build macOS trusts. WavRead is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarised by Apple. The first time you open it you get the ordinary "downloaded from the Internet — are you sure?" confirmation, which says in as many words that Apple checked it for malicious software and none was detected. Click Open and it never asks again. What you will not see is the refusal unsigned apps get, where there is no Open button at all.
