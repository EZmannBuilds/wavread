Payouts

Monthly payouts

Commission is the affiliate's rate of the amount the buyer paid — $15 on a $50 sale at 30% — paid by hand once a month for the month the sales were made (UTC). A month can be paid once every sale in it is past the 30-day refund window, 30 days after the month ends; until then it is on hold and its amount is the total so far. Pay the affiliate first, then mark the month paid here. Its amount and sales are then frozen: a later rate change only affects sales not yet paid.

A sale with any refund is never payable. Before payout, a refund that is not recorded as full is listed as needs review: check it in Stripe. After payout, a refunded sale is listed as clawback needed, with the commission that was paid on it. Nothing here is adjusted automatically, and a recorded payout cannot be changed from this page.
